Pepper Reels Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Pepper Reels is an app for watching short drama series. It is published by Mattia Lieciani ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@nirveo.ai.

The short version

  • You do not create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The app shows no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

Information the app uses

WhatWhyWhere it is kept
Your place in a series and the choices you make in a story To continue where you stopped and to play the story path you chose On your device only
Subscription status and purchase history for Pepper Reels VIP, linked to a random app identifier To unlock VIP episodes and to restore your purchase on a new device With Apple or Google, and with RevenueCat, the service we use to manage subscriptions
Technical information sent when the app loads video or checks a purchase, such as your IP address, device model, operating system version and app version To deliver video to your device and to keep the subscription service working With our video delivery provider and with RevenueCat
Anything you write to us by email To answer you In our email inbox

Services we rely on

  • Apple App Store and Google Play process payments and subscriptions under their own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat records purchases and subscription status so the app knows what to unlock. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • A content delivery network streams the episodes to your device.

These providers handle the information only to supply their service to us.

Subscriptions

Pepper Reels VIP is an auto-renewing weekly subscription. It is billed to your Apple or Google account and renews until you cancel. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

How long information is kept

Information stored on your device stays there until you delete the app. Purchase records are kept for as long as they are needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes your viewing progress and story choices from your device.
  • You can ask us to delete the purchase records held about you by RevenueCat, or ask what information we hold, by writing to hello@nirveo.ai. Tell us you are writing about Pepper Reels.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your personal information under local law. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Pepper Reels is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top. If the changes are significant we will say so in the app.
