I paid for VIP but episodes are still locked

Open the account screen in the app and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple or Google account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel VIP?

VIP is billed by Apple or Google, so it is cancelled in your store account, not in the app.

  • iPhone: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Pepper Reels, Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play, tap your profile picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Pepper Reels, Cancel.

You keep VIP until the end of the week you already paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

An episode will not play

Check your internet connection, then close the app and open it again. If it still fails, write to us and name the series and episode.

Can I change the choice I made in a story?

Not yet. The path you pick is saved on your phone and the story follows it.
